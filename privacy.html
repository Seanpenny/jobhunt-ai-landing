Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Overview

JobHunt AI is a prelaunch product by NexGen Develop. This policy explains how we plan to handle information submitted through the website, waitlist, and future beta experience.

Information We Collect

The landing page may collect your email address when you join the beta waitlist. During beta use, JobHunt AI may process resumes, cover letters, job descriptions, generated drafts, and workflow information that you choose to provide.

How We Use Information

Waitlist emails are used to contact users about beta access, product updates, and launch information. Product inputs are used to generate tailored CVs, cover letters, match insights, and job-search workflow support.

Data Handling

JobHunt AI is being designed with privacy-first document handling, minimal collection, and user control. We do not sell your personal information. You should avoid uploading highly sensitive information unless it is needed for your job application workflow.

User Approval

JobHunt AI does not auto-apply to jobs without your approval. The product helps draft and organize applications, but users remain responsible for reviewing and submitting materials.
