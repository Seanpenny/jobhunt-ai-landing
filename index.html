Trust & Privacy

Your job search data stays under your control.

JobHunt AI is being designed for private, user-approved job application workflows, not silent automation.

Privacy-first document handling

Your resume, cover letters, job descriptions, and generated drafts are treated as sensitive career data. The beta is built around clear user control, minimal collection, and transparent handling.

Clear data handling

Waitlist emails are used to contact beta users. Product inputs are used to generate job-search documents and improve your workflow, not to sell your personal information.

No auto-apply without approval

JobHunt AI helps you prepare stronger applications faster, but you review every CV, cover letter, and opportunity before submitting anything.
